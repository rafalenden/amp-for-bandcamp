Transform your Bandcamp listening experience with Amp for Bandcamp, a Safari extension that adds powerful playback controls and
practical UI improvements to make discovery and listening smoother.

KEY FEATURES

Auto-Play Next Track
– Automatically moves to the next track when playback ends, so listening continues without interruption.

 Keyboard Shortcuts
– Space: Play/pause
– Arrow Up: Previous track
– Arrow Down: Next track
– Arrow Right: Seek forward
– Arrow Left: Seek backward
– W: Add track to wishlist
– Enter: Open track in new tab

BPM Detection
– Display the BPM of a track on album pages

Sticky Album Player
– Keeps the player pinned at the top of album pages while you 

Playback Progress Indicators
– Shows visual playback progress on supported Bandcamp views (including feed and album contexts).

Volume Control
– Set your preferred playback volume from the extension popup.

Leave-Page Playback Warning
– Optional warning helps prevent accidentally leaving Bandcamp while music is still playing.
